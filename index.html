I'm Dhiraj, an infrastructure engineer specializing in Linux systems, virtualization, and cloud operations. Djectory is where that work lives - a practical, no-nonsense approach to keeping infrastructure stable, secure, and automated.

Whether it's a troubleshooting deep-dive, a backup strategy overhaul, or scripting away repetitive ops work, the goal is always the same: systems that just work.
